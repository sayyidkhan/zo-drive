### One sentence about it

Zo Drive replaces the cloud storage, transfer, automation, database,
collaboration, and AI SaaS you pay for each month with one private workspace you
own—complete with an 8B Bonsai LLM running locally on your Zo Computer.

### How did you use Zo?

Built and deployed on Zo Computer: Zo hosts the React web app, Node service,
private storage, and walkthrough video. I also ran the 8B Bonsai local LLM on my
4-core Zo Computer—my first hands-on local-model deployment—to power ZominAI
without sending Drive context to a third-party AI service. The same Zo-owned
Drive powers the browser, CLI, sharing, transfers, Shared Drives, Functions,
Databases, Paste, and ZominAI.
